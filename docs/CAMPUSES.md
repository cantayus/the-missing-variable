# The places and what they are modelled on

Each real place is a slab 96 units across (it was 60). North is −z, east is +x. Distances are squeezed: the buildings a visitor would recognise are there, in the right directions from each other, and the streets between them are left out. Buildings are not to scale with the people.

For each place: the recognisable buildings that are in the model, where they stand in life, and the pages the layout was checked against. A recipe keeps its session rooms, its hidden find and its special props wherever the layout changes.

## Part I places

### yale — Yale University, New Haven
- Old Campus: a quadrangle of Victorian Gothic halls west of the New Haven Green, entered from the Green through Phelps Gate. Connecticut Hall (1750–52, brick) is the oldest building.
- Harkness Tower (216 ft, Gothic, 1921) stands on High Street facing Old Campus, at the south end of its quadrangle (Branford College).
- Sterling Memorial Library closes the west end of Cross Campus. The Beinecke stands just north-east of it on Hewitt Quadrangle, beside the Commons and Woolsey Hall rotunda.
- Hillhouse Avenue and Science Hill (Kline Tower, the tallest building at Yale) lie to the north.
- Checked: yale.edu/about-yale, locations.yale.edu, visitorcenter.yale.edu/tours/architecture-yale, archipedia (Harkness Memorial Tower), Wikipedia "Old Campus".

### harvard — Harvard University, Cambridge
- The Yard. Tercentenary Theatre is the lawn bounded by Widener Library (south, a wide flight of steps and a colonnade), Memorial Church (north, white spire), University Hall (west, white granite, the John Harvard statue in front of it facing Johnston Gate) and Sever Hall (east).
- The Old Yard is the western third, entered by Johnston Gate; Massachusetts Hall and Harvard Hall stand either side of the gate.
- North of the Yard: the Science Center on its plaza, and Memorial Hall (brick Gothic, a tall tower) to the north-east.
- South toward the river: the Georgian houses with their towers (Lowell, Eliot) and the Weeks footbridge over the Charles.
- Checked: Harvard College admissions map, Wikipedia "Harvard Yard" and "Gates of Harvard Yard", Harvard Gazette "The art of architecture", The Crimson's layout guides.

### mit — Massachusetts Institute of Technology, Cambridge
- The Maclaurin Buildings (3, 4 and 10) make a U round Killian Court, open to the Charles on the south. Building 10 carries the Great Dome behind a colonnade.
- Massachusetts Avenue crosses the river on the Harvard Bridge at the west end. Building 7 with the small dome is the entrance on the avenue.
- West of the avenue: Kresge Auditorium (a shell that is an eighth of a sphere, on three points) and the round brick MIT Chapel in its moat, both by Saarinen.
- East: the Green Building, a 21-storey tower by Pei over McDermott Court with Calder's Great Sail; Walker Memorial and the sailing pavilion on the river.
- North-east: the Stata Center (Gehry) on Vassar Street; the Media Lab and Kendall Square beyond.
- Across the river: Boston's Back Bay.
- Checked: MIT campus map (web.mit.edu/facilities/maps), MIT School of Engineering walking tour, Dezeen "The architecture of MIT", tclf.org, Wikipedia "Campus of the Massachusetts Institute of Technology".

### princeton — Princeton University
- Nassau Hall faces Nassau Street across the front lawn behind FitzRandolph Gate. Behind it is Cannon Green, with the twin marble temples of Whig and Clio halls at its far (south) end.
- East of Nassau Hall: Chancellor Green and East Pyne, then Firestone Library and the University Chapel. West: Alexander Hall (Richardson Auditorium) and Blair Hall with Blair Arch, the old way in from the station.
- South-east: Fine Hall tower (mathematics) and the Lewis Library (Gehry) side by side, with Jadwin and the stadium beyond.
- Checked: princeton.edu campus maps (2022–2024), Princeton Alumni Weekly "Features" (2001) on the plan, jerseydigs.com architectural guide, princetonianamuseum.org walking tour.

### illinois — University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign
- The Main Quad runs north to south: the Illini Union at the north end, Foellinger Auditorium (a dome, after Jefferson's rotunda) at the south end, Lincoln Hall at the south-west corner.
- Alma Mater stands at Green and Wright streets at the north-west; Altgeld Hall with its bell tower is just south of her.
- North of Green Street: Engineering Hall, the Bardeen Quad behind it, Grainger Engineering Library on the quad's north side; the Siebel Center and the Beckman Institute further north.
- South of Foellinger: the South Quad with McFarland Bell Tower, the Main Library to the west, Smith Memorial Hall to the east, the Morrow Plots (the oldest experimental field in the Americas) and the Observatory.
- Checked: admissions.illinois.edu photo tour, self-guided quad tour and driving tour; illinois.edu/about/landmarks; Wikipedia "Campus of the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign".

### rensselaer — Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute, Troy
- The campus is on a hill over downtown Troy and the Hudson. The Approach, a granite staircase of 1907, climbs from the city.
- Voorhees Computing Center is a 1933 seminary chapel, the library from 1960 to 1976, a computing center since 1979. Folsom Library (1976) stands where the seminary was. West Hall, once the Troy Hospital, is the westernmost building.
- EMPAC is the glass arts center on the brow of the hill. In life the wooden hull of its concert hall is seen through the glass. Glass is solid in the game, so the hull pushes out through the south front. East of the hill: the '86 Field and the Houston Field House. North: Russell Sage Laboratory and the Pittsburgh Building.
- Checked: archives.rpi.edu building histories (the Approach, Chapel/Voorhees, West Hall), info.rpi.edu/map, Wikipedia "List of RPI buildings" and "Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute".

### gottingen — University of Göttingen
- The market: the Old Town Hall (core of 1270) with the Gänseliesel fountain (1901) in front of it, which new doctors kiss. St. Johannis with its two unequal towers stands just behind; St. Jacobi's tall tower is to the north.
- The Aula on Wilhelmsplatz (classical, 1837) is still the university's main building. The Paulinerkirche held the old library.
- The Wall, the old rampart, is a walk right round the old town. The Gauss–Weber monument stands on it. The observatory Gauss founded is on Geismar Landstraße, south of the town. The Leine canal and the railway station are on the west.
- Checked: uni-goettingen.de (town brochure, "Das meistgeküsste Mädchen der Welt"), de.wikivoyage.org "Göttingen", ADAC maps, Wikipedia "Göttingen".

### maryland — University of Maryland, College Park
- McKeldin Mall runs east to west between McKeldin Library (west) and the Main Administration Building (east), with the ODK fountain down the middle and Testudo in front of the library.
- Memorial Chapel, white columns and a white steeple, is to the south-east. Campus Drive runs across the north with the flower-bed "M"; the Iribe Center on V-shaped supports stands by the main gate on Baltimore Avenue.
- Stamp Student Union and the stadium are to the north-west; Morrill Hall (1898) is the oldest academic building.
- Checked: umd.edu/about/landmarks, cvs.umd.edu campus attractions and map, Wikipedia "McKeldin Mall", SAH Archipedia (University of Maryland).

### edinburgh — University of Edinburgh
- Bristo Square: McEwan Hall, the round domed graduation hall, on its west; Teviot Row House, the oldest purpose-built students' union, on its south; the Informatics Forum, Appleton Tower and the Bayes Centre by Potterrow.
- George Square: the Main Library on the south side, the tower at 40 George Square on the east, the Meadows beyond.
- Old College, a quadrangle under a dome, is on South Bridge to the north-east. The castle on its rock, the Royal Mile and St Giles lie to the north; Arthur's Seat to the east. Greyfriars is just west.
- Checked: ed.ac.uk self-guided tour of the central campus and central area maps, Wikipedia "Bristo Square".

### wisconsin — University of Wisconsin–Madison
- Bascom Hall at the top of Bascom Hill, Lincoln seated in front of it, looking down State Street to the Capitol dome.
- Memorial Union and its terrace of sunburst chairs on Lake Mendota; the Red Gym, a brick armory with castle towers, next door; Library Mall and Memorial Library.
- Also: the Carillon Tower and Washburn Observatory on the hill, Camp Randall stadium to the south-west, Van Hise Hall, Computer Sciences.
- Layout from the university's campus map as I know it; the model keeps the vignette's mirror image, with the hall on the east of its lawn.

### stanford — Stanford University
- Palm Drive ends in the Oval in front of the Main Quad; Memorial Church with its mosaic front is the centrepiece of the Quad, opposite Memorial Court. Arcades of sandstone under red tile run all round.
- Hoover Tower (285 ft) is just east of the Quad, Green Library beside it. The Cantor Arts Center and its Rodin bronzes are west of Palm Drive; the stadium is east of it.
- Also: the Engineering Quad and Gates building to the west, White Plaza, the Dish in the foothills.
- Checked: stanford.edu/life/visit, visit.stanford.edu self-guided tour map, quake06.stanford.edu campus structures, Wikipedia "Main Quad (Stanford University)".

### munich — Munich: LMU, the Technical University and the English Garden
- LMU's main building (Gärtner, 1840) stands on Geschwister-Scholl-Platz, a half-round square with one of two matching bowl fountains, west of Ludwigstraße; the Siegestor is just north along the street, the twin-towered Ludwigskirche and the State Library just south.
- The English Garden lies behind: the Monopteros on its mound, the Chinese Tower and its beer garden, the standing wave on the Eisbach.
- The Technical University's main site with its clock tower is to the west near the Alte Pinakothek. The old town with the Frauenkirche's two onion domes and the town hall on Marienplatz is to the south.
- Checked: lmu.de ("Die LMU & München", location map), muenchen.de and munich.travel on the main building and fountains, ADAC maps on the Siegestor, muenchenunterwegs.de booklet (Chinese Tower, Monopteros, Eisbach wave).

### eth — ETH Zurich
- The main building (Semper 1864, enlarged with its dome by Gull) sits on the edge of the hill above the old town. The Polyterrasse in front of it looks over the city; the Polybahn funicular climbs to it from Central.
- The University of Zurich's main building with its own tower stands next door. Below: the Limmat, the Grossmünster's two towers, the Fraumünster's green spire, the main station across the river, the lake to the south.
- Checked: ethz.ch (Campus Zentrum), zuerich.com (Polyterrasse; main building of the ETH).

### cambridge — University of Cambridge
- The University Library with its tower is west of the river; the colleges line the east bank with their lawns, the Backs, running down to the Cam.
- King's College Chapel; Trinity's Great Court with its fountain and the Wren Library by the river; St John's with the Bridge of Sighs; the Mathematical Bridge at Queens'; the Senate House, Great St Mary's and the Round Church along King's Parade and beyond.
- West Cambridge: the Computer Laboratory in the William Gates Building, the Cavendish Laboratory, and the Centre for Mathematical Sciences with its grass-roofed pavilions.
- Laid out from the university's map as I know it; in the model the river runs east to west, so "north" on the slab is west in life.

### london — London: Bloomsbury and King's Cross
- Bloomsbury: Senate House, Birkbeck, University College behind its portico and dome, the British Museum's colonnade with the round reading room in its court, Russell Square, brick terraces; the BT Tower over Fitzrovia.
- King's Cross: the station's twin sheds, St Pancras with its Gothic front and clock tower beside it, the British Library and the Francis Crick Institute to the west, the Regent's Canal, Granary Square and the gasholder frames north of the canal.
- Laid out from what I know of the two districts; in the model Bloomsbury is on the west and King's Cross on the east, as in life.

### cranfield — Cranfield
- The campus grew out of RAF Cranfield, an airfield opened in 1937; the College of Aeronautics took it over in 1946 and it has its own working airport.
- Kings Norton Library (Foster + Partners, 1993); the Aerospace Integration Research Centre; DARTeC, with a Boeing 737 given by British Airways as a ground demonstrator; a solar farm on the far side of the airfield; the technology park; the village beyond the fields.
- Checked: cranfield.ac.uk (global research airport, aerospace capabilities brochure, campus map, College of Aeronautics history), Central Bedfordshire's Cranfield masterplan, Wikipedia "Cranfield Airport".

### monash — Monash University, Clayton
- The Menzies Building, a tall slab, stands over the Forum lawn with Robert Blackwood Hall, the Sir Louis Matheson Library, the round Religious Centre and the Campus Centre round it.
- Newer: the Learning and Teaching Building by the transport interchange, the Woodside Building, the Chancellery. The Australian Synchrotron is across the road.
- Checked: monash.edu Clayton campus map (2026) and masterplan projects, Monash collections photographs, Wikipedia "Monash University, Clayton campus".

## Part II and III places

### unc — University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill
- From Franklin Street south: McCorkle Place with the Davie Poplar, Old East (the oldest public university building in the country) beside it, the Old Well at its southern end; Cameron Avenue; South Building; Polk Place; Wilson Library and its dome at the far end; South Road; the Morehead-Patterson Bell Tower; Kenan Stadium.
- Also: Morehead Planetarium on Franklin Street, Davis Library and the Pit, Sitterson Hall (computer science), the Carolina Inn.
- In the model the sequence runs the other way up the slab (the library is to the north of the well).
- Checked: unc.edu visitor pages ("Take the picture" tour, Carolina traditions), visitorsguide.unc.edu self-guided tour, Wikipedia "Old Well", the Daily Tar Heel's campus guides.

### columbia — Columbia University, Morningside Heights
- Low Memorial Library, domed, at the highest point; its steps with Alma Mater come down to College Walk, the old 116th Street, which runs from the Broadway gates to the Amsterdam Avenue gates. Butler Library faces Low across the lawns.
- Earl Hall (west) and St. Paul's Chapel (east) sit on Low's cross-axis as a matched pair. North: Uris, Pupin, Schermerhorn. The Thinker stands outside Philosophy Hall.
- Around: Barnard and Riverside Church across Broadway, the Cathedral of St. John the Divine down Amsterdam Avenue, brownstones to the south.
- Checked: visit.columbia.edu walking tour, GSAPP's Morningside Heights self-guided tour, Wikipedia "Low Memorial Library", Columbia University Archives campus tour, Columbia News sculpture walk.

### berkeley — UC Berkeley
- Sather Tower (the Campanile) in the middle; Doe Library and Memorial Glade; Wheeler Hall; Sather Gate leading south to Sproul Plaza, Sproul Hall and Telegraph Avenue; Strawberry Creek.
- North: Hearst Mining Building, Soda Hall. East, up the hill: the Greek Theatre, Memorial Stadium, the Big C, Lawrence Berkeley Lab. West: the bay, with the Golden Gate in line with the tower.
- Laid out from the campus map as I know it; the glade is drawn south of the library, as the earlier vignette had it.

### santafe — Santa Fe Institute
- The Cowan Campus is on a hilltop up Hyde Park Road, in the foothills of the Sangre de Cristo Mountains above the town: one low pueblo-style building, later extended with wings of small shared offices round open common rooms (the "pods", 1998) and a courtyard. On the grounds: Monolith Alpha, and Heliotown II in the old pool house. No public tours.
- The second campus (Miller, in Tesuque) is not shown.
- The town in the corner of the slab is a long way closer than in life: the Plaza with the Palace of the Governors on its north side, and the Cathedral Basilica of St. Francis a block to the east. The plaza and cathedral are from what I know of Santa Fe, not from a search.
- Checked: santafe.edu ("The Two Campuses", "Monastery in the Mountains", Heliotown II, Monoliths, contact and FAQ pages), The Scientist's "The Institute Different".

### cornell — Cornell University
- Central Campus lies between two gorges: Fall Creek on the north (Beebe Lake, the falls, the pedestrian suspension bridge) and Cascadilla Creek on the south. Libe Slope falls away to the west, with West Campus and the War Memorial at the bottom.
- At the top of the slope: Uris Library and McGraw Tower; north of them the Stone Row (Morrill, McGraw and White Halls, fronts facing the slope) with Ezra Cornell's statue; the Arts Quad; Goldwin Smith Hall on its east side with A. D. White's statue; Sibley Hall and its dome on the north; the Johnson Museum (I. M. Pei, 1973) on the corner of the slope.
- South of the tower: Ho Plaza, Willard Straight Hall, Sage Chapel; then the Engineering Quad and Myron Taylor Hall. East of East Avenue: Bailey Hall, the Ag Quad and Mann Library, Barton Hall, Gates Hall, Schoellkopf Field.
- Rewritten so that the gorges are where they are in life (the earlier vignette had one gorge on the west). The east side is squeezed: Gates Hall is really further south-east.
- Checked: Wikipedia "Cornell Central Campus", Cornell campus maps (2013, 2025), E. Kozen's "Architecture at Cornell" page, SAH Archipedia "Cornell University", cornell.edu live view notes, Cornell Chronicle campus history piece.

### ucsd — UC San Diego
- Geisel Library in the middle; Library Walk south from it past the Price Center to the School of Medicine; the Snake Path running east from the library up to Jacobs Hall, with Fallen Star on the corner of its roof; Warren Mall; Atkinson Hall.
- Revelle College in the south-west (Revelle Plaza, Urey Hall); RIMAC in the north; the Blue Line trolley's Central Campus station and Interstate 5 on the east.
- West of North Torrey Pines Road: the Salk Institute, the gliderport, the cliffs and the ocean; Scripps Institution of Oceanography and its pier.
- The Stuart Collection sculptures (Sun God, the La Jolla Project, Bear) are works of art, so they are named on signs where they stand but not modelled. The trolley is drawn at ground level; on campus it runs on a viaduct.
- Checked: UC San Diego self-guided tour maps (2021, with trolley), Stuart Collection brochure and UC San Diego Today pieces, the "campus transformation" slideshow, Wikiwand "Geisel Library".

### toronto — University of Toronto, St. George campus
- Robarts Library at St. George and Harbord; east of St. George Street, University College facing King's College Circle, Convocation Hall on the circle, Knox College, Hart House and Soldiers' Tower, the Back Campus; the Ontario Legislative Building in Queen's Park to the south-east.
- Bloor Street on the north (Varsity Stadium, the Royal Ontario Museum, Philosopher's Walk running south from it past Trinity College); College Street on the south with its streetcar; Spadina Avenue on the west with its streetcar and One Spadina Crescent standing in the middle of the road; the CN Tower to the south.
- West of St. George: Sidney Smith Hall, the Bahen Centre, McLennan Physical Labs, New College.
- Rewritten: the streetcar used to run between the library and the college, where there is no line.
- Checked: U of T convocation "campus locations" page, the 3D campus map (2019), the self-guided tour (2018), the Landmark Project documents, Wikipedia "University of Toronto St. George" and the list of buildings, the Cultural Landscape Foundation's entry.

### manchester — University of Manchester
- The Old Quadrangle on Oxford Road (Waterhouse): Whitworth Hall, the John Owens Building, the tower over the arch; the Manchester Museum next door. Behind: the Rutherford and Coupland Buildings, the Alan Gilbert Learning Commons, the Main Library, the Samuel Alexander Building.
- Across Oxford Road: the Kilburn Building, University Place (the drum), the Aquatics Centre, Brunswick Park, the Alan Turing Building, the Nancy Rothwell Building, the National Graphene Institute, the Students' Union.
- Towards town: the Mancunian Way flyover, the railway and Oxford Road station, the Sackville Street Building and Sackville Gardens with the Turing memorial bench, the Refuge clock tower. The other way: the Whitworth and its park.
- The model is turned a quarter: Oxford Road, which runs north to south, crosses the slab from west (the Whitworth end) to east (the town end).
- Checked: manchester.ac.uk (historic buildings, "our campus", 360 tours, "finding your way"), the university's heritage stories, Wikipedia "Whitworth Building", the alumni blog "Our campus: then and now".

### cmu — Carnegie Mellon University
- The Mall runs from Hamerschlag Hall at the west end to the College of Fine Arts at the east, with Baker and Porter Halls along its south side. The Cut comes down to it from Forbes Avenue, the Fence where they meet; Gates and Hillman on the west of the Cut, Purnell and the Cohon University Center on the east, the Pausch Bridge between.
- Also: Wean Hall, Newell-Simon Hall, Hunt Library, Margaret Morrison, Gesling Stadium, the Tepper Quad across Forbes Avenue.
- Around: Panther Hollow and its railway to the west, the Carnegie Museums and Pitt's Cathedral of Learning up Forbes Avenue, Schenley Park with Phipps Conservatory and Flagstaff Hill to the south.
- Checked: CMU campus maps (2017–2021), the self-guided tour page, the admission map notes, CMU's campus art piece.

### berlin — Berlin
- Unter den Linden from the Brandenburg Gate and Pariser Platz east to the Schlossbrücke: on the north side Humboldt University (the brothers' statues at its gate, Frederick the Great on horseback in the middle of the avenue), the Neue Wache, the Zeughaus; on the south side Bebelplatz with the State Opera, St. Hedwig's Cathedral and the Old Library; Gendarmenmarkt a few streets south.
- Over the bridge, Museum Island: the Lustgarten, the Altes Museum, the Berliner Dom, the Humboldt Forum. North-east across the river: Alexanderplatz with the World Clock, the Rotes Rathaus, the Fernsehturm. West: the Reichstag on the bend of the Spree, the Tiergarten, Potsdamer Platz.
- The courtyard where Clever Hans was shown is in Griebenowstraße, by the Zionskirche, in the north of the city. It is a good deal further from the avenue than the model has it.
- Rewritten: the university used to be on the wrong side of the avenue and the gate stood away from it.
- Checked: berlin.de and visitberlin.de pages on Unter den Linden, Wikipedia and Wikivoyage "Unter den Linden", a walking-tour guide. The courtyard's street and the church are from what I know, not from a search.

### montreal — Montréal: Mila and the mountain
- Mila is in Mile-Ex, in a converted building at 6650–6666 rue Saint-Urbain (opened January 2019), in the old garment district between Mile End, Little Italy and Parc-Extension. Nearby: the railway, Jean-Talon Market, Parc Jarry, streets of row houses with outside staircases.
- On the mountain: the cross on the summit; the Université de Montréal's main pavilion (Roger-Gaudry) with its tower; Saint Joseph's Oratory and its steps. Far to the east, the Olympic Stadium and its leaning tower.
- The model looks at the mountain from the north side and pulls everything in close. In life the railway runs between Mile End and Mile-Ex; here it is behind the row houses.
- Checked: mila.quebec (contact, student life), the city's and Newswire's notes on the 2019 opening, the O Mile-Ex leasing brochure, Wikipedia "Université de Montréal". The Oratory, market, park and stadium are from what I know.

### amsterdam — Amsterdam and the Science Park
- Amsterdam Science Park is on the eastern edge of the city, just inside the A10 ring, with its own railway station. On it: the UvA Faculty of Science (Science Park 904), LAB42, CWI (Science Park 123), Nikhef, AMOLF, Amsterdam University College, student housing, data centres, Anna's Tuin en Ruigte and the Polder café.
- The city: canals lined with gabled houses, the Westerkerk and its crowned tower, Centraal Station on the harbour, the De Gooyer windmill, the Magere Brug.
- The city and the park are several kilometres apart; the model puts them side by side. The Magere Brug really crosses the Amstel.
- Checked: UvA route descriptions and campus pages, the Science Park visitor maps (2010–2023), cwi.nl visitor information, Wikipedia "Amsterdam Science Park". The city landmarks are from what I know.

## Company places

### microsoft — Microsoft, Redmond
- The rebuilt East Campus: four- and five-storey offices in groups round a two-acre central plaza, car-free, with playing fields and a thermal energy centre. 156th Avenue NE runs along its west side.
- Redmond Technology station (light rail) sits between the avenue and State Route 520; the footbridge with the white peaked canopy crosses the highway from the station to the older West Campus (The Commons).
- The earlier vignette had the highway on the wrong side of the campus; it is now on the west, where it is.
- Checked: Microsoft's own pieces on the modernisation and the bridge, the Urban Land Institute profile, the Daily Journal of Commerce, The Urbanist's station-area piece, Wikipedia "Microsoft campus". The cricket pitch is from what I remember of the coverage.

### google — Google, Mountain View
- Bay View: three canopy buildings (two for work, one for events) with the "dragonscale" solar roofs, on land leased from NASA Ames, east of Stevens Creek. Hangar One at Moffett Field stands beside it; the Bay Trail runs along the marsh.
- West of the creek: the Googleplex (with the dinosaur skeleton and the volleyball court), Charleston East, Shoreline Amphitheatre. US 101 along the south.
- The earlier vignette had the older campus on the east; it has moved across the creek.
- Checked: Google's Bay View opening post, Wikipedia "Googleplex", ArchDaily, World-Architects, the Mountain View Voice / Almanac piece, 9to5Google.

### apple — Apple Park, Cupertino
- Bounded by Interstate 280 (south), Wolfe Road (west), Homestead Road (north) and North Tantau Avenue (east). The ring in the middle with its orchard, meadow and pond; the theatre on a hill; the fitness centre; long car parks under solar roofs along the freeway; the visitor centre and research buildings across Tantau Avenue; the old Glendenning Barn, moved and kept.
- Where exactly the barn and the fitness centre stand inside the site is from memory.
- Checked: the City of Cupertino's Apple Park pages, Apple's February 2017 press release, Wikipedia "Apple Park".

### nvidia — Nvidia, Santa Clara
- Endeavor (2017) and Voyager (2022) side by side on San Tomas Expressway: the long sides back on to a channelised creek, the pointed ends face the expressway. Between them a four-acre park under a trellis dotted with solar panels. Older company buildings and car parks are across the expressway, reached by a footbridge.
- The site is under the approach to San José airport.
- Checked: Metropolis magazine on the campus, Tom's Hardware on Voyager, Nvidia's media page, The Real Deal and CoStar on the planned third building (not drawn).

### ibm — IBM Research, Yorktown Heights
- The Thomas J. Watson Research Center (Eero Saarinen, 1961) on Kitchawan Road: one long crescent, three storeys, glass along the front and fieldstone behind, set on a wooded hill. The Taconic State Parkway passes close by.
- The crescent has been turned so that the glass front is the outside of the curve, which is how I remember the aerial photographs; the search results did not settle it.
- Checked: Wikipedia "Thomas J. Watson Research Center", IBM's history page on its architecture, the CLUI land-use database entry.

### belllabs — Bell Labs, Holmdel
- The Holmdel complex (Saarinen, 1962; now Bell Works) at 101 Crawfords Corner Road: a mirrored glass box with a cross-shaped atrium, in the middle of an oval drive, with ponds in front and car parks at either end. The three-legged water tower by the entrance; a copy of Karl Jansky's rotating antenna in the north-east of the oval. The horn antenna is really on Crawford Hill, a couple of miles off.
- Checked: Wikipedia "Bell Labs Holmdel Complex", the Cultural Landscape Foundation's pages, the National Register nomination, the Library of Congress survey record.

### amazon — Amazon, Seattle
- The Spheres on Lenora Street between 6th and 7th Avenues, under the Day 1 tower; Doppler and the other towers round about; the dog park; the South Lake Union streetcar. Denny Park and Lake Union to the north; the Space Needle and the monorail to the north-west.
- The streetcar is closer to the Spheres than in life, and the monorail is drawn as one straight beam. The plants inside the Spheres show as green panes among the blue, since glass is solid in the game.
- Checked: Wikipedia "Amazon Spheres" and "Day 1 (building)", Amazon's headquarters tour pages, SAH Archipedia, amazon.jobs on the Seattle campus.

### netflix — Netflix, Los Gatos
- Low cream-walled, tile-roofed buildings at Winchester Circle and the taller ones on Albright Way, off Winchester Boulevard, near the Los Gatos Creek Trail. Vasona Lake County Park is a little way up the creek, with the Billy Jones Wildcat Railroad (a miniature steam line) beside it. Highway 17 to the east.
- The lake and the railway are closer than in life, and both are from what I know; the mountains are drawn to the north because that is the far side of the slab.
- Checked: Netflix's Los Gatos jobs page, property listings for 100–150 Winchester Circle, the Los Gatos Patch piece on the Albright Way expansion.

### meta — Meta, Menlo Park
- South of Bayfront Expressway: MPK 20 and MPK 21 (Frank Gehry), each a single long building with a park on its roof, joined by the sunken court called the Bowl; a yellow bridge for bicycles and walkers over the expressway to the Bay Trail and Bedwell Bayfront Park. Behind, an old rail corridor and the houses of Belle Haven. Willow Road on the east.
- North of the expressway: the older campus at 1 Hacker Way, then salt flats and the bay. The expressway runs east on to the Dumbarton Bridge.
- The earlier vignette had the marsh directly behind the long building; the expressway and the older campus are now between them.
- Checked: Meta's MPK 21 announcement, the City of Menlo Park's project and park pages, Palo Alto Online and the Almanac on the bridge, the ASLA award page for MPK 20, ArchDaily.

### sf — San Francisco
- On the bay side from north to south: Coit Tower, the Ferry Building, the Bay Bridge, Oracle Park at China Basin, then Mission Bay with the UCSF campus and Chase Center, and the Caltrain terminus at 4th and King. Downtown: the Transamerica Pyramid and Salesforce Tower. Further off: the Golden Gate Bridge, the Painted Ladies on Alamo Square, Sutro Tower.
- Distances are squeezed a great deal: the Golden Gate is five miles from Mission Bay.
- Checked: the Muni system map, BART station-area maps, Wikipedia "Mission Bay, San Francisco", the Bay Trail map of the southern waterfront, the city's railyards map.

## The home town

### deckle — Deckle
- Invented, so there was nothing to check. It has grown to the same size as the other places: the river and the railway run the full width, and there is now a High Street with a market square, a church and a school, a goods shed, Station Road, a green with a bandstand, allotments, a farm across the river and a hill with a beacon behind the mill.
